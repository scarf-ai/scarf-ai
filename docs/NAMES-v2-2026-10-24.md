## Rejected
- Taken on Pons: DIBS (16), GLINT (19), NIBB, SNAGG, KIPPO, PIKKO, NOKI, MOKO, NIKKA, KLEPTO, CROWE (also a famous actor's surname).
- CAWS — CAW is the name of Cobo Agentic Wallet, a wallet for AI agents: a direct conflict in the niche.
- KAWK — reads as a rude word in English.
- CORVA — all domains taken.
- KAAW, SKRAW, PILFR, VRIK, GRIBB, TINKO, OBBY, ZIBB, RIKKO, TAKKO — available, but weaker in meaning or sound.

## Why SKARF
- The name and the image reinforce each other: see the scarf, remember the name, and vice versa.
- The second meaning ("scarf down" — to sweep up greedily) describes an agent that grabs memecoins, mints, raffles and bets.
- 5 letters, read the same way by everyone, the ticker matches the name.
- 0 matches on Pons and DexScreener; @skarf_ai and all three skarfai.* domains are available.
- Downside: the short @skarf is taken, so the handle has the _ai suffix.
